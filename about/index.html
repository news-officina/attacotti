About Attacotti

In 367 the Attacotti joined the Picts and the Scots in the raids that harried Roman Britain; the Roman historian Ammianus Marcellinus names them among the raiders. Nobody knows for certain where they came from, only that it was beyond Rome's frontier. This Attacotti comes from beyond the Wall too. It is written in the voice of a Scot who left after Brexit and now watches from Brussels, where Scotland should have a seat of its own.

It is against Brexit and for an independent Scotland back in the European Union, and it says so plainly. It takes no government or newsroom at its word, not London's, not Brussels', not Holyrood's: it asks who benefits, finds the document and names the source. It is an independent publication and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any party or movement, nor by any independence campaign.

Articles here are drafted with AI from public sources. An editor checks most of them before they appear; an article that went out unchecked says so. Illustrations are generated with AI. The voice is a persona, not a person. Attacotti is independent and speaks for no party or movement, for or against independence.
